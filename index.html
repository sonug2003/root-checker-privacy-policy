🛡️ Privacy Policy for Root Checker

Last Updated: January 13, 2026

Quick Summary: Root Checker detects if your Android device is rooted. All root checks happen locally on your device. We use Google AdMob (ads) and Firebase Analytics which collect Device IDs, IP address, and usage data. You can opt-out anytime via Privacy Settings. Users must be 13+ to use this app.

2. Your Rights & How to Opt-Out

🚫 Right to Opt-Out of Data "Sale": Under CCPA, VCDPA, and similar laws, sharing data with Google for personalized ads may constitute a "sale." You have the right to opt-out:
  1. In-App: Privacy Settings → "Do Not Sell My Info"
  2. Device: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  3. Email: pyrbit0supp@gmail.com (Subject: "Opt-Out Request")

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Age Requirement: Users must be 13 years or older. On first launch, users must confirm their age. If under 13, the app immediately closes with zero data collection.

If your child bypassed age verification, contact us at pyrbit0supp@gmail.com to delete their data.
