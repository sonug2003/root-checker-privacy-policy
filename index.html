🛡️ Privacy Policy – Root Checker: NextGen 2026

Effective Date: March 4, 2026  ·  Developer: Alok Ranjan & Himanshu Ranjan

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Alok Ranjan and Himanshu Ranjan ("we", "us", "our") handle information in connection with the Android application Root Checker: NextGen 2026 ("the App") available on Google Play.

The App's core function — checking whether your Android device is rooted — runs entirely on your device and requires no internet connection to perform these checks. No root‑check result, device state, or personal details are transmitted to our servers.

However, the App does include third-party SDKs for advertising and analytics that collect limited data as described below. By using the App you agree to the practices in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We (the developer) do not run any backend server or database. We do not directly see, store, or process your personal data. The data categories below are collected by the third-party SDKs embedded in the App.

2a. Data collected automatically by third-party SDKs

  • Advertising ID (AAID) — Android's resettable advertising identifier, used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads. Collection is subject to your consent (via the in-app consent prompt on first launch).
  • Device & app information — device model, OS version, language, app version, and similar technical attributes collected by AdMob and Firebase Analytics to operate ads and aggregate analytics.
  • IP address — collected transiently by Google's ad and analytics servers when the App makes network requests. We do not store it.
  • App usage events — anonymised in-app events (e.g., screen views, feature interactions) collected by Firebase Analytics to help us understand how users use the App.
  • Consent preferences — your ad-consent choice is stored locally on your device by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

2b. Data NOT collected

  • Root check results or device root status — processed entirely on-device and never sent anywhere.
  • Name, email address, or any contact details (we have no account or sign-up system).
  • Location data (no location permission is requested).
  • Camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or files.
  • Crash logs or diagnostics — the App does not include a crash-reporting SDK.

2c. Purchase information

If you purchase the Premium upgrade, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive only a non-personally identifiable purchase token confirming the purchase status. We do not see or store your payment card, billing address, or Google account details.

3. How We Use Information

  • Serve ads — AdMob uses the Advertising ID and device information to display relevant advertisements to free-tier users.
  • Measure ad performance — AdMob reports aggregate ad metrics (impressions, clicks) to us; these are not tied to your identity.
  • Improve the App — Firebase Analytics provides aggregate, anonymised usage statistics (e.g., which features are used most) so we can prioritise improvements.
  • Verify purchases — the purchase token from Google Play Billing is used to unlock Premium features and is not used for any other purpose.
  • Consent compliance — UMP stores your ad-consent choice to ensure ads are served appropriately under GDPR and similar regulations.

5. Advertising (Including Rewarded Ads)

The free version of the App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, which may include banner ads and rewarded video ads. AdMob may use the Advertising ID and device information to show you personalised ads based on your interests.

On first launch (or in regions where required by law), the App shows a consent prompt powered by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). If you decline personalised ads, non-personalised ads will be shown instead. Non-personalised ads still use contextual signals but do not use the Advertising ID for Interest-based targeting.

How to opt out of personalised advertising

  • Via the App: A consent/privacy settings option is accessible from the App's settings menu, allowing you to update your ad-preference choice at any time.
  • Via Android Settings: Go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (path varies by Android version) to reset your Advertising ID or opt out of interest-based ads system-wide.
  • Via Google: Visit adssettings.google.com to manage Google ad personalisation.

6. Purchases (Premium Upgrade)

The App offers a one-time Premium upgrade through Google Play In-App Billing. All payment processing is handled by Google Play; we do not collect, see, or store your payment card number, billing address, or full Google account information.

Upon a successful purchase, Google Play sends our App a purchase token. We use this token only to verify that Premium has been purchased and to unlock the relevant features on your device. The token is not linked to any personally identifiable information on our end.

For refund, billing disputes, or subscription management, please use the Google Play app or visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

7. Data Retention

Because we do not operate a backend database, we do not retain your personal data ourselves. Retention is governed by each third-party service:

  • Firebase Analytics: Event data is retained for up to 14 months by default (Google's standard retention period), after which it is automatically deleted by Google.
  • Google AdMob / ad-serving logs: Retained by Google per their Privacy Policy.
  • Consent preferences (UMP): Stored locally on your device until you reset or reinstall the App, or update your consent choice.
  • Purchase token: Stored locally in the App's private storage until you uninstall the App. Google Play retains purchase records per their own policies.

8. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the App and its data flow. The App's core root-detection logic operates entirely offline, and no sensitive detection results leave your device.

All network communication (for ads and analytics) uses HTTPS/TLS encryption provided by the respective Google SDKs. No plain-text transmission of any data values occurs through the App itself.

Because we do not maintain a server database containing your data, there is no central repository that could be breached to expose your information.

9. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable under local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The App is designed for Android users who are exploring device root status, which is a technical topic generally relevant to adult or teenage users. If you believe a child has used the App and data has been inadvertently collected by our third-party SDKs, please contact us at pyrbit0supp@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action.

10. Your Rights & Choices

Access & Deletion

Since we do not hold a database of your personal data, there is no profile to retrieve or delete on our end. To request deletion of any data associated with your use of our App (including Firebase Analytics events), please email us at pyrbit0supp@gmail.com with subject line "Data Deletion Request – Root Checker". We will forward the request to the relevant Google service or assist you in completing it directly.

Advertising ID

You can reset, limit, or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. This takes effect immediately and prevents AdMob from linking future ad interactions to your previous ID.

Firebase Analytics

You can disable Firebase Analytics data collection by opting out via Android Settings → Google → Ads & Measurement (exact path varies by device). You can also request deletion of your analytics data by contacting us.

Consent Choice (EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in a region covered by GDPR or similar regulation, you can change your ad-consent choice at any time from the App's settings menu.

Uninstall

Uninstalling the App from your device will delete all locally stored data (consent preferences, purchase token) immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the Effective Date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will provide an in-app notification or a notice on our website.

Continued use of the App after the Effective Date of any updated policy constitutes your acceptance of the revised terms. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
