3. How the App Works

Root Checker performs local device checks to determine if your Android device has root access. Here's what happens:

  • Checks for su binary files in common system locations
  • Looks for known root management applications
  • Examines system build properties
  • Tests system partition write access
  • Checks for root-related paths and files

All these checks are performed locally on your device. No information is sent anywhere.

📱 What is Android Privacy Sandbox? (2026 Update)

Google's Privacy Sandbox on Android is a major privacy initiative introduced in Android 13+ and required for all apps by 2026. It replaces traditional ad tracking with privacy-preserving alternatives:

  • Old Way (Pre-2026): Apps tracked you across different apps and websites using persistent identifiers
  • New Way (Privacy Sandbox): Your device processes data locally and shares only anonymized, aggregated information

The 3 Privacy Sandbox Permissions Explained:

  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID: Privacy-friendly ad ID (not shared across apps like old tracking)
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION: Measures ad effectiveness without tracking individuals (aggregated data only)
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS: Interest-based ads without tracking your browsing history (topics like "Sports", "Tech" determined on-device)

Your Privacy Controls:

  • Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Android Settings → Privacy → Ad Topics → Manage topics (view/block interest topics)
  • Or use Root Checker's in-app Privacy Settings

More Info: Android Privacy Sandbox Documentation

  • How we use it: Root Checker integrates with Play Age Signals API to receive age-appropriate signals from Google Play

  • Layer 3: In-app age gate dialog (manual confirmation)
